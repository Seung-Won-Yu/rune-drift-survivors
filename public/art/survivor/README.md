# Ash & Amber prototype art

These are generated prototype assets, not a finalized animation production pack.

- `hero-ash.png`, `hero-ember.png`, `hero-grove.png`: dedicated 1254 × 1254 RGBA companion atlases, 4 × 3. Four walk poses, four attack/cast poses, two hurt poses and two defeat poses. [Prompts and extraction provenance](./HERO-PROMPTS.md).
- `player.png` (style lab reference only, no longer loaded by the game): 1254 × 1254 RGBA, 4 × 4 atlas. The cartoon walk/attack animations occupy rows 3 and 4. The first two rows retain the pixel comparison. Original prompt and generation notes: [style lab prompt](../../../docs/design/survivor-restart/style-lab/PROMPT.md).
- `enemies.png`: 1448 × 1086 RGBA, 4 × 3 atlas. Four walking poses per row: mushroom imp, forest hound, forest brute. Generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-09-08, referencing the player atlas for visual style. Original alpha PNG copied without raster editing.
- `ash-sovereign.png`: 1774 × 887 RGBA, 4 × 2 boss atlas. Separate walk and cast poses with a branched crown, ivory mask and burgundy mantle. [Generation and alpha-extraction prompts](./ASH-SOVEREIGN-PROMPT.md).
- Ground, shadows, gems, weapon effects and HUD icons are drawn in Canvas/CSS/SVG in the repository.

Enemy generation prompt:

> Create one production-ready cartoon enemy animation atlas for a 2D top-down fantasy survivor game. PNG with REAL transparent alpha background, no painted checkerboard, no scenery, no text, no borders. Exact evenly spaced grid of 4 columns by 3 rows, 12 separate sprites total, each entire creature occupies at most 70% of its cell width and height with generous transparent padding. Row 1: four walk keyframes of a small mischievous forest imp: squat plum-purple mushroom head, pale masklike face, two tiny pointed ears, little dark root feet, amber eyes. Row 2: four walk keyframes of a lean charcoal forest hound with long pointed ears, rust-orange mane, four legs, cream muzzle, low forward body, distinct animal silhouette. Row 3: four walk keyframes of a stout moss-armored forest brute: broad green shoulders, tan bark face, two branch horns, stone club in one hand, heavy short legs, no shield. All face right and slightly toward viewer, same 3/4 elevated GAME sprite perspective, no isometric tiles. Each row one identical creature with changing leg pose and body turn, no changes to identity/equipment between frames. Style: match the clean bold dark outlines, two-tone cel shading and expressive compact fantasy proportions of the cartoon character in the BOTTOM HALF of the supplied reference. Reference only for art style, do not reproduce the human. Cute but dangerous forest creatures, readable at 40 screen pixels. No pixel art, no realistic rendering, no glow, no shadow under feet. Anchor every creature's feet at 85% of its own cell height, body centered at 50% cell width. Four poses: stride right legs forward, down passing, stride opposite, up passing. Keep all appendages INSIDE cell.

Animation limitations: four poses per action, horizontal mirroring rather than full directional animations, some frame-to-frame proportion and foot-anchor variation. `render.js` uses measured crop boundaries for the player's attack row to avoid neighboring-frame bleed. The game now loads the dedicated companion sheets. Their measured row crops and proportional drawing preserve feet and body proportions. Minor cloak/sword-tip cropping remains near a few attack boundaries.
