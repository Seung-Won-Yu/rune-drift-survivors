## Feedback ladder

1. Dormant: low, cool-toned ground ring.
2. Next but locked: colored core and low beam; HUD shows unlock time.
3. Ready: full-height beam, stronger pulse, HUD shows `READY`.
4. Channeling: expanding charge ring and local `SEAL` signal.
5. Complete: reward burst, circuit count update, next beam takes ownership.

## Failure clarity

- Reaching a future seal early does not channel it; it remains visibly dormant.
- Reaching the next seal before unlock shows its remaining time in the HUD.
- Leaving the active radius drains partial channel progress instead of resetting instantly.
- Missing a seal does not end the run, but reduces the result score and leaves the circuit incomplete.
